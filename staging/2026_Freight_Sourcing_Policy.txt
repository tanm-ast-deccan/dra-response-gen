NOVARETAIL - GLOBAL FREIGHT PROCUREMENT POLICY
Date: Jan 20, 2026
Subject: ZBB Budgeting Rules for Ocean Freight

1. FIXED CONTRACTS:
For all lanes where a fixed contract rate is offered, the rate includes all base freight and fuel surcharges (BAF). Multiply the annual TEUs by the Fixed Contract Rate to determine the total liability.

2. SPOT MARKET FUEL SURCHARGES:
Spot market rates only cover base freight. Analysts must calculate the fuel surcharge manually. 
For standard long-term forecasting models, corporate finance uses a locked internal baseline for marine fuel:
- Standard Marine Fuel Placeholder: $400.00 USD per Metric Ton.

**CRITICAL ZBB EXCEPTION FOR EU LANES**: 
Due to extreme volatility in the Red Sea and Suez Canal, analysts calculating Spot Market feasibility for ANY lane terminating in Europe (e.g., Rotterdam, Hamburg) are FORBIDDEN from using the $400 placeholder. 

You must query the live web for the current, real-time "VLSFO (Very Low Sulphur Fuel Oil) Global 20 Ports Average" price in USD per Metric Ton. Apply this live VLSFO spot price to calculate the fuel surcharge. Any model utilizing the $400 internal placeholder for an EU-bound lane will fail audit.
